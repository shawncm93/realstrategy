Privacy policy

Real Strategy LLC. Effective October 21, 2026.

This policy covers the website at realstrategy.co. It describes what the site collects, what we do with it, and who else can see it. Information you give us after you become a client is covered separately by the privacy notice in your engagement letter.

What we collect

The site has one form, on the contact section of the home page. If you use it, you give us your first and last name, your email address, and, if you choose, your phone number, a description of your business type, and a message. The form does not send that information to a server we run. It opens your own email program with a message addressed to shawn.choo@realstrategy.co, and nothing is sent until you press send in that program.

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and has no advertising or tracking scripts. Fonts and images are served from the site itself.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply to you and to set up a call. We keep the email in our mailbox the way any business keeps correspondence. We do not sell it, rent it, or add you to a marketing list.

Who else can see it

  • GitHub, Inc. hosts the site through GitHub Pages. Like any web host, it records server logs, which can include your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. GitHub's handling of those logs is described in its own privacy statement.
  • Our email provider stores the messages you send to shawn.choo@realstrategy.co, as it does all our email.

No one else receives information from this site.

Reviewing or deleting what you sent

Email shawn.choo@realstrategy.co and ask. We will tell you what we have from you and delete it on request, unless we are required to keep it as part of a client file.

Do Not Track

The site does not track visitors, so a Do Not Track signal from your browser changes nothing. We do not respond to it because there is nothing to turn off.

Children

The site is for business owners and is not directed at anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.
